About

Style and substance

Studio Teknabu is an independent company with a clear purpose: to create digital experiences people can trust and control.

Style and substance belong together. Considered design gives an experience clarity and character. A useful purpose gives that care direction. The aim is work that feels coherent, communicates clearly and makes sense for the people it is intended to serve.

That care belongs in the details: language, structure and the choices that help an experience feel clear and complete.

The starting point is the whole problem: what needs to be understood, how the pieces connect and who benefits. That wider view helps shape the details, from the way something looks to how it works and what it asks of someone.

I’m Sammy John Rawlinson, the founder of Studio Teknabu. I set the direction and take responsibility for the work. Building an independent company gives me room to pursue that vision with ambition and care.
